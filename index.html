Our Story

Learn the Art of
Baking with Joy

Welcome to Cake Science — where the love of baking meets the joy of teaching.
~ Cake Science ~ is a Bakery & Confectionery Center located in Sri Vijaya Puram, Andaman Islands. It brings the joy of baking in everyone and is open to all age groups for both male & female for the ONE who loves to learn baking with fun & passion.

🧁

Passion for Baking

Every class is crafted with love and deep expertise in baking science — from chemistry of cakes to the art of decoration.

📚

Hands-On Teaching Experience

With 5+ years of professional teaching, our instructor guides you step by step until you feel completely confident.

🌸

Friendly & Hygienic Environment

Our studio is warm, welcoming, and spotlessly clean — the perfect space to learn, create, and enjoy.

🚀

Supporting Your Journey
