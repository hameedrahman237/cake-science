About Cake Science

Nestled in the beautiful Andaman Islands, Cake Science is dedicated to bringing the joy of baking to everyone. Our hands-on classes combine traditional techniques with modern artistry, ensuring every student leaves with real skills and delicious creations. We believe baking is both a science and an art — and we're here to teach you both.
